VayuDrishti

AI for Cleaner Cities

VayuDrishti is a Pathway-powered real-time AI system that autonomously reduces urban air pollution by optimizing traffic signals using live traffic and AQI data.

About VayuDrishti

VayuDrishti focuses on reducing pollution at traffic intersections where vehicle idling causes maximum emissions. Using real-time data streams, the system continuously recalculates pollution cost and dynamically adjusts signal timings to minimize emissions — not just congestion.
