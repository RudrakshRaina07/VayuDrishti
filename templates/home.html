AI for Cleaner Cities

VayuDrishti is a real‑time AI traffic intelligence system that reduces pollution by dynamically optimizing traffic signals using live AQI and traffic density data.

About VayuDrishti

VayuDrishti focuses on reducing emissions at traffic intersections where vehicle idling produces the highest pollution. Using continuous real‑time data streams, the system recalculates pollution impact and adjusts signal timing autonomously.

Unlike traditional congestion‑based systems, VayuDrishti makes pollution‑aware decisions that help cities move toward cleaner air without requiring new infrastructure.
